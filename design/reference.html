Warm, roomy and considered. For something a customer chose to open: room to breathe, a serif with an opinion, and surfaces that read like paper rather than panels.

This is a style reference, not a page to copy. Match the tokens, spacing and component shapes below. Do not reuse this page's wording, sections or layout. Your application has its own content and structure.

Using this reference
  • Lift the custom properties from :root verbatim. Do not invent adjacent shades.
  • Keep the room: 17px body, 8px spacing base, and generous vertical gaps between sections.
  • Keep the serif for display type and the sans for everything else. The pairing is the theme.
  • Buttons and badges are pill-shaped; containers use 14px corners.
  • Every interactive element keeps a visible focus ring, in the green rather than the accent.
